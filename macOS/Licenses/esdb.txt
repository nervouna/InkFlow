InkFlow takes the spellings and capitalization of its English dictionary from the
ESDB Hunspell dictionaries en_US-large and en_GB-large, version 2026.02.25 (word
list size 70):

  hunspell-en_US-large-2026.02.25.zip
    SHA-256 06ab5a2a12c29033f100988d3b0a5e53dcad40bf2473ccb78270619d1da99321
  hunspell-en_GB-large-2026.02.25.zip
    SHA-256 704a49438a3a9d4f11cd76db433a6b230b9ce8770ee13fa28700052c4517ddc1

Modifications by InkFlow: expand the Hunspell prefixes and suffixes of both
dictionaries into word forms, keep the forms made of ASCII letters, apostrophes,
hyphens and periods, attach wordfreq frequencies to them
(Core/Data/english-wordfreq.tsv, see wordfreq.txt), and generate the English and
mixed dictionaries from the forms that pass the frequency gate, adding the
Capitalized and ALL-CAPS display forms of admitted lowercase words. The recipe is
macOS/scripts/snapshot-english-frequency.py and Core/scripts/prepare-rime.sh.
