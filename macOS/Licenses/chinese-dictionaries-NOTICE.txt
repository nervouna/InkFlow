InkFlow modifications: normalize explicitly supplied Pinyin whitespace, case and
ü/v; merge term/reading pairs in source priority; retain Frost weights; calibrate
new Ice and legacy entries to positive-weight Frost overlaps; apply reviewed
local corrections. No pronunciation generation or simplified/traditional conversion.

The bundled dictionary-manifest.json records exact commits, files (by catalog
identifier), source checksums, generation recipe and applied calibration. The
pinned legacy compatibility input is included as legacy-pinyin-simp.dict.yaml.
Source generation code is in macOS/Sources/DictionaryGenerator.swift and the
pinned source catalog is in macOS/Sources/DictionaryModels.swift.

Default-enabled domain additions:

  Frost commit. Original numeric weights are retained only for missing pairs.

  The repository declares MIT, Copyright (c) 2025 Jingchao; see rime-selected.txt.
  That notice is the conversion repository's declaration. No explicit original

  establish independent clearance of the upstream data's licensing chain.
  The 10,300 two-column rows match the official SCEL's term/reading pairs.
  InkFlow assigns the source-declared absent weight 1, fills missing pairs after
  the original union, and corrects the exact reading of 串行打印机 from
  chuan hang da yin ji to chuan xing da yin ji.
- A small app-owned Chinese supplement selects 14 verified terms from Sogou
  https://pinyin.sogou.com/dict/detail/index/133021 and

  is made. Additional modern technical terms use explicit InkFlow readings.

Chinese update checks follow the pinned paths in the Frost, Ice and rime-selected
repositories, including the conversion repository rather than Sogou's service.
The small curated Chinese/English vocabulary is updated with the application.
This implementation was prepared for local personal use; publication is separate.
