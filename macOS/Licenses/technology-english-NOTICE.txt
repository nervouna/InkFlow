InkFlow selected 104 exact display spellings from that source, retained their
capitalization, spaces and punctuation, and supplied lowercase ASCII lookup
aliases (including cpp for C++, csharp for C#, and dotnet for .NET).
Eighteen additional display spellings are explicitly maintained by InkFlow:
AI, Type-C, eBPF, Swift, AppKit, UIKit, SwiftData, Git, Docker, Docker Compose,
Python, Rust, Java, Dart, Flutter, React, Gemini and RAG. These are not attributed
to en_ext. The 122-row source and per-word provenance are macOS/Data/english-technology.tsv
and macOS/Data/english-technology-provenance.tsv in the source repository.

The selected text/code pairs are merged with the unchanged rime-easy-en input,
retaining its existing aliases and deduplicating identical pairs. Admission and
weights use the shared wordfreq/override policy. Explicit 4.0 policy overrides
for 111 exact display spellings are product decisions, not measured frequencies.
Eleven existing observations at or above the gate are preserved. The resulting
English and mixed dictionaries also carry their existing easy-en/wordfreq
attribution; see easy-en-LGPL-3.0.txt, easy-en-GPL-3.0.txt and wordfreq.txt.
